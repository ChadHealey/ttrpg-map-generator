macro anchors. Fixed sampled indices declare each role's contiguous exposed coast interval, root,
far crosscut and whole-disk witness before fitting. The remaining coast plus those exact root
chords defines B. A fixed bay interval declares its existing radial geodesic mouth and witness.

The partition helper rejects overlapping intervals and malformed witnesses. The unchanged private
certificate must still prove simple/disjoint polygons, valid crosscuts and collars, whole disks,
exact stitching, every adopted dimension/share, protected water and the complete owner guard.
Independent design review confirms that this ordering is compatible with issue 171. A continuous
curve alone does not establish acceptable macro anatomy; the actual six-row images decide.

Each literal layout declares bounded regional anchor offsets from owner-scoped random streams
before sampling. These change broad coast regions, not only rotation or island placement.
The finite candidate table and all failed attempts remain visible. A balanced-only compactness

It cannot shrink the certified cap, change owner quotas or relax a geometric threshold.

member-specific orientation independent of the coast normal. Every member remains paid before
body fitting. Its actual oriented radius determines the proposed offset, and all site/body/island

variant order and the 256-vertex limit remain bounded. Exhaustion is an explicit failure.

## A measured limitation of simple taper recipes

Independent arithmetic explains why simply narrowing every peninsula toward a tip is not a
sufficient repair. For a root-normal, nonflaring planar strip with root width w and height h,
area is at most w·h; the stricter w·h/2 bound requires a triangular/linear taper envelope.
The retained sufficient certificate imposes w/c≤.16 and maximum planar extent e/c≤.45, with
h≤e and c≤1. The respective upper areas are therefore .072c² and .036c² steradians.
Normal-01's quota .13106846473029043 with paid-island fraction .0095 requires at least
`.05*4π*(1−.0095)*q = .08157039410355812` steradians for its peninsula. Even the nonflaring
strip bound is insufficient. A flared, lateral or curved layout outside that restricted class
is necessary for this retained case. This is not a proof that the adopted targets or all natural
peninsulas are impossible, and it does not authorize weakening those targets.

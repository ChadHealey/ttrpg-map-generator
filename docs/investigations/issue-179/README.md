# Issue 179 — tapered continental coasts and varied bays

This experiment applies the independently reviewed [far-crosscut width certificate](../issue-178/README.md)
to three complete coast layouts. Broad-root peninsulas replace the pinched terminal heads of
[issue 177](../issue-177/README.md). All adopted targets, exact paid quotas, public controls,
finite budgets and frozen placement/field machinery remain unchanged.

The [experiment contract](experiment.md) permits at most two immutable six-row comparisons.
[Local findings](local-findings.md) retain material variants, numeric failures and captured
source/receipt/image evidence. All six complete paid owner sets pass readiness before each matrix;
three ordinary literal layouts survive. That inventory is not visual proof.

## First comparison

[Results](comparison-r1/results.json) and [source manifest](comparison-r1/manifest.json) retain
all six numeric successes, exact local repeats and twelve native/half images. The largest absolute
preview water error is `.018580` percentage points; full error is `.003076`. Every preview owner
passes its `.25/count` tolerance. Full owner measurements remain diagnostics.

The [independent visual review](visual-review.md) is mixed. Normal-01/02 retain R3 failures from
layout A's flat-lipped, round-ended bay. Normal-03/04 pass provisionally with concerns; the balanced
control retains repeated-mouth concerns and the fragmented control is provisionally interpretable.
The main task inspected all twelve images and agrees that the common A bay needs a targeted repair.
The former pinched-head defect is materially improved, so the prior blanket R2/R3 rejection is
not carried forward automatically. No family or human selection follows from this first matrix.

## Second revision

The [second revision](revision-r2.md) changes only A's declared bay into an oblique asymmetric
opening. The constructor preserves all random scope strings, B/C/subordinate shapes, member sites,
controls and quota formulas. Local comparison verifies all 20 non-A owners are byte-for-byte equal;
the seven A owners change their bay and uniform quota refit while retaining every original quota.

The [second runner](run-r2.mjs) captures its own full source closure and revision record, leaving
all first-revision runtime/evidence frozen. All six [second comparison rows](comparison-r2/results.json)
pass numeric gates and exact local repeats. The largest absolute preview water error is `.026604`
percentage points; full error is `.003300`. Every preview owner passes its unchanged tolerance.

The [independent second visual review](visual-review-r2.md) provisionally passes all four ordinary
rows with concerns. The targeted A bay no longer has parallel flat lips, but its pointed recess is
still conspicuous. The balanced control retains R3: repeated mouth-and-point outlines, including
blunt nearly parallel bay lips, remain visible at half size. The fragmented control remains
provisionally interpretable. The main task separately inspected all twelve second-comparison images
and agrees with the withheld family recommendation. Repeated pointed notches remain a morphology
concern even where an individual ordinary row passes provisionally.

## Disposition

**Completed experiment; no family selection.** Two immutable comparisons exhaust this issue's
allowance. Four provisional ordinary passes are useful progress, but the balanced control prevents
a complete-family recommendation. Human decisions remain pending. The independent
[evidence review](independent-review-r2.md) verifies the retained second revision; passing numeric
or source checks does not change the visual result.

The next bounded work is [issue 180](https://github.com/ChadHealey/ttrpg-map-generator/issues/180),
a frozen-candidate seed/control audit, and
[issue 181](https://github.com/ChadHealey/ttrpg-map-generator/issues/181), the proposed production
contract and compatibility sequence. Broader control failures or support limitations should be
known before another bay-only visual repair. Neither authorizes production adoption or relaxation
of the targets. A future visual revision requires a separate bounded issue.

All work remains private investigation code. Accepted v1/v2, production, dependencies and old
evidence stay unchanged. All commits remain local; **no Git push**.

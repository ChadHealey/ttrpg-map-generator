# Issue 187 — bounded whole-coast A prototype

This investigation tests whether coauthoring the complete broad body and lower-lobe root can
remove the opposing-mouth geometry rejected in issue 184. The [literal design](design.md)
specifies all 29 central anchors, role and witness identities, and two regional displacement
tables. It is one A prototype, not a new whole-world family or production generator.

The [independent literal review](independent-design-review.md) and main-task clearance preceded
the first execution. The [first immutable state](evidence/state-1/summary.json) passes all
60 certificates, but the author and main task reject its local image for the remaining obvious
opposing pointed mouth (R3). Numeric feasibility does not resolve that failure.

The experiment permitted two complete, predeclared material states, each
tested on exactly 60 body-only cases: three existing paid-body quota expressions, the anatomy
center and four corners, and four variant amplitudes. Both executed states and all outcomes are
retained. A second state requires an explicit diagnosis and design review; there is no third.

The adopted [issue 167 targets](../issue-167/README.md), frozen
[issue 178 certificate](../issue-178/certificates.mjs), and
[issue 172 sampler/partition](../issue-172/coast-partition.mjs) remain unchanged. No additional
random stream, island placement, 134-case sweep or world render is authorized. A local numeric
pass cannot establish visual success, family selection or human acceptance.

The [harness plan](harness-plan.md) describes immutable capture, trusted replay and local image
verification. See the [first-state disposition](disposition.md), [verification](verification.md),
[native panel](evidence/state-1/panel.png) and [half panel](evidence/state-1/panel-half.png).
The [final second state](evidence/state-2/summary.json) also passes all 60 certificates, but
its continued lower bank ends in a triangular arm opposing the upper lip. The author, main task
and [independent final review](independent-local-review-state2.md) reject R3. Both permitted
states are spent; no third state or world experiment follows from this result.
See its [complete literal design](states/state-2/design.md),
[native panel](evidence/state-2/panel.png) and [half panel](evidence/state-2/panel-half.png).

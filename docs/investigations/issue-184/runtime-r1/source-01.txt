# Issue 184 bounded bay and shoulder experiment

This private experiment tests whether authored asymmetric bay banks and adjacent
shoulder transitions resolve the geometric excavation recorded by
[issue 183](../issue-183/visual-review.md). The complete nine-row family is the
observable outcome. The [visual contract](../issue-164/visual-contract.md) and
[adopted targets](../issue-167/README.md) are unchanged.

Only the exposed A, B and large-primary bay/shoulder intervals may change.
Actual role polygons, roots, far cuts, collar disks and interior witnesses remain
exact before fitting. Explicitly declared bay-mouth coordinates and bay witnesses
may change before fitting, with every certificate rerun. C and subordinate geometry
remain frozen. A removed island site must have a replacement edge declared before
execution; six sites, existing attempts and category payments remain bounded.
The [first design](design.md) explains the initial proposed geometry; retained
local findings and later declarations record any permitted subsequent state.

At most three complete combined A/B/large geometry states may execute. Each
material state retains its sources, every tested certificate including failures,
and a local panel before another edit. These are three total states, not separate
allowances for each layout. Local diagnostics are neither world evidence nor
human acceptance. Main-task and independent local inspection must find a credible
macro change before the world gate opens; numeric success is insufficient.

The constructor retains sixteen candidates, original private seed scopes, complete
paid owners, a single body quota fit, exact category payments and the frozen
certificate, placement and field algorithms. The revised large branch receives
truthful metadata; it is not described as unchanged issue-182 output. No target,
role reclassification, chart limit, random algorithm, renderer, production source,
accepted evidence or ocean/polar/fragmentation behavior changes are authorized.

Before each world matrix, all exact 134 inputs from issue 183 must actually
certify and place, with strict repeats and complete receipts. The four ordinary
retained rows must still expose A, B and C. Each of the three named recovered
rows must expose the large branch. A failure closes the world gate; a successful
subset or cap estimate cannot replace this cohort.

At most two new immutable nine-row matrices may execute. Their exact input order
is normal-01 through normal-04, connected-majority, fragmented-islands,
default-001, default-004 and default-006, with unchanged seeds and controls from
[issue 183's corpus](../issue-183/corpus.mjs). The second matrix requires a
completed retained first matrix and a predeclared revision-r2 document. It binds
the first manifest/results bytes. A failed row stays visible; no seed is replaced.
No fourth geometry state is permitted merely because a matrix allowance remains.

The runner snapshots transitive runtime/input sources and declared documents
before the gate, repeats each actual row and image, retains individual results
immediately, and checks final source integrity. Existing preview/native/half
profiles, zero contour, coverage tolerances, preview-owner gate, guard gaps and
sampled seam/pole/shared-anchor checks remain unchanged. Full-owner coverage is
diagnostic. A source/hash verifier is not a rerender, continuous-topology proof,
formal interval certificate or cross-platform comparison.

Both the main task and a separate reviewer inspect all eighteen exact images per
matrix against every positive and R1–R6. A provisional family recommendation
requires all seven ordinary rows to pass and both controls to be interpretable;
reviewer differences remain explicit. Human visual selection, accepted ADR,
production realization, complete controls and macOS/Linux evidence remain pending.
Known [control limitations](../issue-180/findings.md) are not cured by this work.

A need to change role geometry, support mathematics, target values, C or
subordinates, production behavior, or the stated state/matrix bounds ends this
experiment with a concrete result and a separately bounded successor. No push.

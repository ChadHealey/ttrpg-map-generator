# Issue 172 — direct continental coasts and general collars

**Completed rejected experiment; no v3 selected.** This applies the independently reviewed
[issue-171 design](../issue-171/design.md) after the [issue-170 rejection](../issue-170/README.md),
under the unchanged [adopted targets](../issue-167/README.md). All work and commits remain local;
GitHub issues record progress, but nothing is pushed.

The new private [certificate](certificate-notes.md) measures a declared coastline through a
root-adjacent general collar. It retains exact area accounting, topology, full interior disks,
feature shares, peninsula/bay dimensions, detached islands and envelope clearance. Independent
review found no actionable certificate defect. Direct coast construction was tested separately;
a valid collar example does not establish an acceptable continent or world.

The [experiment contract](experiment.md) requires complete construction of the exact six retained
inputs, at least three accepted ordinary layouts and all known applicable local packing bounds
before rendering. It permits at most two immutable comparisons with source/input/image hashes,
exact repeats, numeric coverage gates and independent native/half visual review. Failed geometry
and failed images remain evidence. Human visual decisions and production C1–C3 remain pending.

Both [r1](comparison-r1/results.json) and [r2](comparison-r2/results.json) pass all six numeric rows,
including exact paid quotas, complete local certificates, necessary packing bounds, finite
placement, preview/full coverage and local repeat checks. The three ordinary layout families
survive in both revisions. This resolves the specific local readiness failures in issue 170.

It does not resolve visual morphology. The main task and independent reviewer each inspected all
24 native/half images. Both revisions are provisionally rejected on every row for repeated feature
silhouettes and geometric excavations or slab-like bodies (R2/R3). R2 improves smooth coast joins
and island size, shape and orientation; those improvements are explicitly retained in the
[independent r2 review](visual-review-independent-r2.md), alongside the
[r1 review](visual-review-independent-r1.md). No human decision is inferred.

[Verification](verification.md) records 107 passing focused tests, independently reviewed
predicates, replayable local failures, immutable comparisons and the broader check limitation.
The [r2 design](revision-r2.md) also proves why one restricted straight nonflaring peninsula class
cannot meet the largest retained quota; that is not a universal impossibility result.

The smallest justified next investigation is a bounded design for nonradial geodesic bay mouths.
The radial-mouth restriction belongs to this sufficient constructor/certificate, not the adopted
167 targets. A successor must prove area, continuous topology and depth before implementation;
removing this restriction is a hypothesis for improving coast freedom, not a promised visual fix.
No third issue-172 comparison, production integration or target relaxation follows from this result.

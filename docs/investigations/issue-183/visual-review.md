inspected all nine native PNGs and all nine half-size PNGs in
[comparison-r1](comparison-r1/results.json). All numeric gates pass. Three local
material states were spent before this first matrix; the unused second matrix
does not authorize another geometry state.

unsampled coast vertices produce a conspicuous angular excavation at world scale.
The restored A bay and frozen large-primary bay retain long, blunt lips in some
orientations. These are actual polygon margins, not raster or seam defects.
The complete family does not satisfy the unchanged

Across all rows, no decisive R1 ribbon, R4 regular global arrangement or R6
cosmetic rescue was observed. Axes, spacing, facing margins and broad ocean
openings vary. Islands generally remain attached visually to specific margins;
small groups in the island-heavy row carry the stated R5 concern. Native pixels
do not hide failures at half size. The one-primary rows represent genuine
construction recovery, but recovery is not visual acceptance.

The next bounded hypothesis is to author asymmetric curved transitions through
the protected bay coast, including its shoulders, while preserving the exact
role polygons and all adopted targets. B needs actual curve samples in place of
its straight segments; A and the large branch need their long lips addressed as
well. Merely increasing raster resolution or adding edge noise is insufficient.
No such fourth recipe is implemented under this issue. Any follow-up must keep
these nine inputs and the full 134-input readiness gate, retain failures, and
independently review the complete family again.

The [independent review](independent-world-review.md) independently rejects
normal-02, normal-03, connected-majority and default-004. It is less severe on
normal-04, default-001 and default-006 because their seam/polar views limit the
strength of a standalone failure judgment. Those differences are retained; both
reviews reject the complete family.
Neither review selects a production family or supplies the required human decision.

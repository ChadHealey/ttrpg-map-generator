# Component expressiveness demonstrated

Both predeclared component fixtures pass the full applicable subordinate certificate. The
peninsula fixture credits the lower bay bank to P; the reflected lobe fixture credits the upper
bank to its lobe. Each connected bay shore crosses a declared root endpoint internally while
its mouth shoulder belongs to an exposed role coast, away from a root junction. The same
literal candidates fail the frozen B-only certificate's `bay-coast` rule.

The result establishes the intended private expressiveness change. All positive-body checks,
original root/far/disk identities, applicable angular targets and quota accounting remain active.
The new proof uses exact shared exterior edges on the stitched body; it does not forgive
arbitrary role/water contact. The intended planar precut body has the pocket reattached once,
while the surviving body quota excludes that water.

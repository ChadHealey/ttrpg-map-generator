## Exact frozen finite recipe

Three independently authored coast modules replace the prior shear-based layout identity.
A has a northern mass, western lobe and southeast peninsula; B has a southwest mass,
northeast lobe and northwest peninsula; C has a northern shelf, southern taper and westward
peninsula. Roots and far-crosscut vertices belong to the authored polygons before fitting.
Every interior, role, bay and collar witness receives the same declared polar transform and
uniform quota scale. No post-stitch smoothing, role relocation, feature shear or global identity
warp is applied.

The anatomy seed stream selects a starting layout. Twelve ordered candidate slots comprise three
cyclic layouts with four island-site variants each. Each member has its own seed/owner/kind/index/
variant stream for starting anchor, traversal direction and irregular polygon phase. A variant
also selects the fixed allowable island margin `[0, .015, .03, .05]` beyond the maximum body
vertex radius, in post-fit chart units. It never changes paid area or a certificate threshold.

B/C declare six actual exposed coast edges. A and subordinate bodies use six fixed cumulative
arclength fractions `.03, .20, .39, .59, .77, .97` of their lower coast (both endpoints below
`y = -.20`, maximum endpoint `x < .25`). Each island tries at most six anchors times the four
outward offsets `.015, .03, .05, .08`. The actual rotated seven-vertex polygon's bounding radius
sets its offset from the coast. Every candidate site must fit the declared body-relative radius
limit and be disjoint from body, bay and prior islands. Exhaustion is explicit; no member is dropped.

Isolated-island and archipelago areas are paid independently from owner quota. Within each
category, unequal member weights are `1 / (1 + .65 * index)`. Raw radial perturbation is
`1 + .13 * sin(2.3 * index + phase)` with radial/tangential axes `.4` and `1.2`. Each polygon
receives an exact direct area fit, then site rotation/translation. Body area is fitted to the
remaining quota before any island is placed.

After a complete successful certificate, six-owner balanced inputs additionally reject guards
above `(π/2 - .05) / 2`. These receipts retain `certificateOk: true` and use the separate
`balanced-guard-preference` selection code. The construction never changes the certificate
result or relabels this preference as a failed geometry proof. A/C exceed this preference in the
final balanced row and fall through to B within the same twelve-slot budget.

## Controls and limitations

The shared validated control mapping is reused from issue 170:

| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Circumference         | Kilometres per radian = circumference / 2π; local spherical geometry is unchanged.                                                                                                                              |
| Water                 | Exact total land fraction = 1 − water / 100.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Continent count       | Exact owner slot count, 1–8.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Distribution          | Existing squared template-size quotas and half-maximum primary classification.                                                                                                                                  |
| Fragmentation         | Four bands `min(3, floor(percent / 26))`; A's authored bay tip changes by `.003 * band`. B/C retain their fixed coast definitions. This is a narrow geometric mapping, not established fragmentation semantics. |
| Island abundance      | Zero removes the category; otherwise `ceil(percent / 25)` members, paid share `.02 * percent / 100`.                                                                                                            |
| Archipelago abundance | Zero removes the category; otherwise `max(2, ceil(percent / 15))` members, paid share `.01 * percent / 100`.                                                                                                    |
| Ocean connectivity    | Validated and retained as requested metadata; local geometry does not establish the requested topology.                                                                                                         |
| Polar character       | Before exact area fitting, multiply local y by `.96`, `1` or `1.04` for ocean-biased, neutral or land-biased. No scalar override. The retained six inputs are neutral and do not prove polar semantics.         |

No claim is made that every valid control input is geometrically realizable. Too-small primary
quotas exhaust their finite budget with actual predicate failures. Local witnesses, exact quotas,
finite bounds and successful samples do not establish cross-platform arithmetic identity.

# Issue 177 local construction evidence

All six retained inputs construct their complete paid owner sets with the frozen issue-176
certificate. This is a local geometry result. It does not establish world placement, the visual
contract, human acceptance, ocean connectivity or production readiness.

## Recipe and bounded changes

Each primary starts as one periodic sampled coast, partitioned at fixed internal root and far-cut
indices. Layout A uses 28 anchors, three samples per interval and tension .12. Its two lobe roots
cross broad continuing flanks. The initial A peninsula peak at `[-.78,.28]` was reduced to
`[-.78,.155]`, and its neighbor `[-.92,.15]` to `[-.92,.09]`; the .13 root remains unchanged.
The central peninsula body share fell from about 7.15% to 5.66%, preserving the adopted floor.
Layout B and C use independently authored broad flanks, with their final literal geometry in
[layout-b.mjs](layout-b.mjs) and [layout-c.mjs](layout-c.mjs). Their smaller asymmetric peninsulas
retain fixed role identities. No coast is smoothed after partition or quota fitting.

The constructor tries at most 12 candidates: three literal layouts, each with four owner-anatomy
attenuations `1,.85,.6,.3`. The seed-scoped layout rotation plus primary rank distributes the
first choices cyclically. Subordinates use their own rank and three compact, asymmetric eight-anchor
coasts, sampled four times per interval with tension .12; one has a broad shallow inlet. Two
subordinate shoulder anchors move by at most .055 and .04 from the owner anatomy stream. Their
explicit interior witness is the origin. All choices precede area fitting and certification.

The frozen issue-170 control recipe retains all nine public controls and original validation.
Legacy squared template sizes determine each exact quota and primary classification. The local
polar Y stretch transforms every role, root, far cut, disk, bay and island anchor, followed by
uniform area fitting. The bay `mouthKind` remains `wedge-geodesic`. No scalar polar override is used.
Ocean choices remain metadata until separately proved.

Each detached category is paid independently: island share `.02 × abundance/100` and archipelago
share `.01 × abundance/100`. A zero abundance removes only that category. Counts remain the frozen
recipe's maximum four islands and seven archipelago members. Each category's member weights are
`1/(1+1.2i)^1.4`, normalized to its exact payment. A finite three-polygon library supplies irregular
six/seven-vertex shapes; each member has a fixed owner-scoped shape, orientation and cyclic site
order before checking at most six actual coast edges × four offsets (`.015,.035,.065,.1`).
The actual rotated polygon bounds its placement offset. Every member must be disjoint from body,
bay and previously paid members. No failed member is dropped.

For the six-balanced control only, a complete successful geometry certificate above
`(π/2−.05)/2 = .7603981633974483` is separately rejected as `balanced-guard-preference` before the
next candidate. This preference does not modify the certificate, quota or adopted thresholds.

## Reproduction and verification

The recommended command is read-only and reconstructs from each frozen source closure, comparing
all receipts and PNG bytes in memory. It also checks artifact hashes and renderer dependencies:

```sh
for stage in initial failed-b pre-diversity b-extent-rejected b-share-rejected b-share-corner-rejected final; do
  node docs/investigations/issue-177/verify-local.mjs "$stage"
done
```

The [writer](write-local.mjs) and [repair-stage writer](write-repair-stages.mjs) are provenance tools:
they create a new directory and deliberately fail if it already exists. They never update retained
stages. Their hashes are included in the relevant manifests. The focused common/A tests verify
six-row deterministic construction, exact payments, original size quotas, all role certificates,
finite site/vertex limits, ordinary layout inventory, cyclic preferences, distinct subordinate
coasts, invalid-input rejection and preserved wedge semantics. Human acceptance remains pending.

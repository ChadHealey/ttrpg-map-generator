# Issue 175 independent design review

The final [water-wedge design](design.md) has no remaining actionable mathematical
or implementation-boundary finding. A bounded private predicate and adversarial
tests are justified. This review does not accept a constructor, a world image, or
v3 production adoption.

## Proof checked

For the normalized ordered mouth, d=det(a,b)>0 makes the angular cone the positive
cone generated by a and b. Positive spherical interpolation coefficients put the
minor arc in that cone. The separate outward-bowing result from
[issue 173](../issue-173/design.md) supplies its strictly seaward position. Both
facts are necessary: the cone condition alone does not establish which side of
the chord contains the lens. Their intersection is convex and contains the lens.

The boundary-to-interior exclusion argument is valid for each bounded positive
polygon independently. Along v=a+b, the derivatives of the unnormalized wedge
forms are d, d and 2d. In particular, the linear derivative
ell(v)=det(b-a,v)=-2d. A ray starting inside a polygon and in W must eventually
exit that polygon at positive time, strictly seaward of the chord. This boundary
exit cannot be a shoulder. The argument needs no inverse chart evaluation along
the part of the planar ray outside the chart disk.

Keeping the existing planar pocket and witness on the origin side preserves their
separation from the outward lens. Existing pocket/body topology and exclusion of
all positive components then justify the unchanged area, opening and depth lower
bounds. The witness distance bounds the adopted maximum pocket depth from below;
it is not a measurement of that maximum. The primary removal floor still uses the
paid body quota, and the lens adds no credited area.

## Finite boundary checked

The proposed segment clipping checks whole segments. Omitting a near-constant
affine constraint enlarges the possible intersection, which can reject additional
valid inputs but cannot conceal a crossing in real arithmetic. Rejecting contact,
nonfinite arithmetic and unresolved interval separation is consistent with the
specified finite budget. Checking vertices alone would be unsound: the retained
segment from (.8,-.3) to (.8,.3) crosses W despite both endpoints lying outside it.

Two details were resolved before this final review:

- Shoulder edges use exact structural identity and an active face that is strictly
  negative at the opposite endpoint. That affine function is negative along the
  entire open edge. Expanded-wedge clipping applies only to nonincident edges;
  applying it as a singleton-shoulder requirement would reject valid incident
  edges. Roles and islands receive no shoulder waiver.
- The incident-direction expression uses linear ell(v), not affine L(v). The
  documented entering edge from (.65,-.12) to (.66,-.12) has ell(v)=-.0024 but
  L(v)=.1536, demonstrating the false acceptance caused by that substitution.

The EPS expansion and parameter slack remain ordinary binary64 diagnostic
assurance, as stated in the design. They are not a formal outward-rounded
certificate. The unbounded wedge is also deliberately larger than the finite
lens, so valid land outside the lens can still be conservatively rejected.

## Corroboration and next boundary

Independently checked the positive example's simple body, pocket and pre-cut body,
its witness locations, and boundary intersections limited to four structural
shoulder endpoints. The two seaward vertices violate the old global support rule
while avoiding W. Its body area is 1.0624 sr, planar pocket area is .05935 sr,
opening bounds are approximately [.2088827422,.2757527950], depth lower bound is
.2001792946, and ratio lower bound is .7259375. These satisfy the illustrated bay
targets, including comparison with the largest retained removal floor; this is
not a complete primary candidate.

Replayed the read-only [corroboration script](corroborate.mjs) and compared its
parsed output with [the retained JSON](corroboration.json) using strict deep
equality. They match exactly. The script's clipping examples support the arithmetic
checks; they do not replace the proposed conservative predicate or its tests.

The successor should implement only the explicit private mode and the design's
positive and negative cases, preserving the existing radial default and rejecting
failed wedge inputs without fallback. Whole-segment crossing, face tangency,
incident entering edges, extra component contacts, reversed mouth/ring handling,
near-parallel clipping and unchanged primary floor execution are material test
requirements. Constructor and visual evaluation remain separate work.

## Other required observations

All seven ordinary rows show one to three visually primary masses. The balanced control's six
co-primaries are interpreted as a control outcome, not silently renamed to meet the ordinary
count. Its required internal hierarchy is still assessed for every body.

Axes, positions and facing margins vary across the retained rows and the three recovered rows.
Broad open water remains coherent, and the visible spaces between owners are not uniformly
constant-width channels. The balanced control has a noticeable two-band arrangement, an R4
concern, but the independent decisive finding is its repeated excavation geometry rather than
an unsupported assertion of equal spherical spacing. These raw masks do not establish ocean
enum semantics or production water-basin realization.

Island groups generally have unequal sizes and occur near particular margins. The fragmented
control has a short bead-like arc along the right edge of its left subordinate and another
small arc around the upper-right companion. These are localized R5 concerns; they do not
independently dominate that entire control. The other rows do not show a decisive atlas-wide
necklace pattern. R1 is not independently triggered: broad polar bands and broad bodies with
tapering projections are distinguished from thin, repeatedly necked chains. No additional
noise or overlays have been introduced to claim a repair; small islands do not cure the R3
failures, and no separate cosmetic change supplies an independent R6 finding.

The broad bodies, water spaces and unequal island groups remain visible at half size. So do
the sharp cuts in the failing rows. Resolution is not the cause of, or a remedy for, those
macro failures.

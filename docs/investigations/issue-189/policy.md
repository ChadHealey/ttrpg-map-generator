Public controls remain exactly:

- Water: integer 45..80 percent.
- Count: integer 1..8.
- Distribution: balanced, varied, oneDominant.
- Fragmentation/island/archipelago percentages: integer 0..100, independently.
- Ocean: singleGlobal, connectedMajority, multipleBasins.
- Polar: oceanBiased, neutral, landBiased.

Use public `parseAtlasControls` on unknown input, then retain public `validateAtlasControls` diagnostics on its typed result for the domain; a geometrically unsupported valid control tuple must not be called malformed input.

For this family every primary bay requires alpha <= 1.4. Its convex LAEA image disk contains every positive body and island vertex, hence every edge/interior, with exact area-preserving chart payment. Therefore each whole owner's fraction satisfies q <= (1-cos(1.4))/2 = 0.41501642854987947. This includes detached paid islands, not just the body or B interior. The sole owner is primary by construction. At count 1 and water 45, q=.55; the deficit is .13498357145012058. Water 58 also fails (q=.42). At least 58.49835714501206 percent water is necessary, so the first permitted integer not excluded by this bound is 59. Water 59 and 80 are not thereby proved feasible: morphology, disks, island sites and guards can still fail.

Retain an analytic receipt containing the validated tuple, exact symbolic inequality, binary64 displayed values, certificate source pin and outcome `valid-input / unsupported-family-capacity / constructor-not-attempted`. No candidate, sampling, rendering or replay of geometry for these analytically excluded rows. This is not an impossibility theorem for the adopted targets; a different chart/whole-owner representation could remove the restriction.

A second source-level caution: a semantic continent requires at least 20% of retained land area. Consequently no accepted partition can contain more than five semantic continents under policy 1. Public continentCountIntent up to 8 is an intent, and private paid owners are not semantic continents. D3 must explicitly preserve that distinction rather than promise eight policy-1 continents or silently reinterpret existing semantic types. Verify wording against the owning product contract; if product intends exact semantic count, record that independent contract conflict before implementation.

For other rows, a constructor failure exhausts only the existing finite budget and keeps all attempts; placement failure keeps its bounded receipt. Neither is proof of universal geometric impossibility.

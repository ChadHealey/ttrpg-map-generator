# D3 findings and contract disposition

The fixed family fails the declared polar and fragmentation comparisons. The two paired
`multipleBasins` requests yield truthful unsupported outcomes from the unchanged public policy.
Coverage and source geometry succeed on the placed rows, but they do not establish control
intent or extracted-role survival. No v3 proposal is selected.

This document follows the immutable [policy](policy.md), [corpus](corpus.mjs) and
[decision](evidence-r1/decision.json). The subject is issue-184 material revision
`issue-184-curved-bays-r3`, captured by its `comparison-r2`; it is not D1's earlier checkpoint.
The policy and implementation notes describe the pre-execution checkpoint and remain unchanged.

### Conditional failure is already part of the adopted contract

Issue 167 explicitly permits valid-input no-proposal for a demonstrated cap, feature or water
conflict, missing certificate, coverage failure or exhausted finite budget, with the failed
obligation reported. It also rejects an implicit all-inputs-fail policy as a product proof.
See the [adopted conditional contract](../issue-167/README.md) and
[production contract](../issue-181/production-contract.md).

Accordingly, `fullPublicDomainSupported: false` in the decision is a scope observation, not a
new requirement that every public tuple must produce geometry. The frozen policy's phrase
“first required construction design” for a sole owner of area fraction .55 applies **only if
extending this family to that tuple**. It is not an additional mandatory v3 gate. The existing
validator must accept these valid controls; a truthful conditional no-proposal does not require
clamping the domain or replacing the adopted targets.

## Ocean intent through unchanged semantics

For both fixed seeds, the ocean enums share exactly the same geometry and full land/water mask.
Each requested public semantic mode was nevertheless evaluated. Both `singleGlobal` and
`connectedMajority` return one allocated open region, one basin root and one open graph component
containing 100% of open marine weight. One allocated region leaves **zero or one clearance core**
ambiguous. Both `multipleBasins` requests return `semantic-mode-unsupported` because the policy
cannot obtain two disconnected basin roots. That establishes fewer than two cores, not an exact
private core count. See the [seed-1](evidence-r1/paired-1-ocean-multiple.json) and
[seed-2](evidence-r1/paired-2-ocean-multiple.json) receipts.

The relevant public rule is clearance strictly greater than 16 graph edges within the largest
raw water component. Its deterministic allocation can produce multiple open regions even when
raw water is connected. The measured single-global boundary rows demonstrate this: distribution
balanced has three allocated open regions and two enclosed seas; one-dominant has ten open
regions and no enclosed seas; both abundances at 100 has four open regions and three enclosed
seas. In each case the returned open regions form one connected graph with one basin root.
These rows were not additional `multipleBasins` probes and are not reported as tested successes
for that mode.

Thus ocean's absent geometry effect is not a universal semantic no-op or a theorem that
`multipleBasins` is impossible. The fixed paired masks do not realize that mode. Explicit
unsupported outcomes are permitted. If support for those cases is required, a separately scoped
constructor must reserve suitable marine clearance structure using ocean intent; relabeling
water or changing the public policy inside this discovery would not solve the stated contract.

## Exact successor contract and stop

Keep public controls and accepted schemas unchanged in this investigation. Malformed records
fail public parsing; valid but unsupported outcomes keep their requested tuple, failed obligation,
attempt ledger and `proposalPresent: false`. The released generation result union is
`proposed-full | cancelled | invalid`; `failed` is a diagnostic stage, not another public status.
A future adapter may return `invalid` with an explicit unsupported-family diagnostic while the
input remains valid, or propose a separately reviewed public unsupported status. No mapping is
implemented here and existing warning-only connectivity proxy behavior is unchanged.

Issue 181's unaccepted macro schema-2 proposal adds water as the eighth macro control because
construction pays it upstream. This investigation establishes that unchanged semantics does not
realize `multipleBasins` on the two paired masks. If the selected support contract requires
construction to change those masks for ocean intent, add ocean as the ninth explicit field to
that still-unaccepted schema 2, retain matching water/ocean classification provenance, and make
ocean edits invalidate macro and its downstream products. Do not invent schema 3 merely to
revise an unaccepted proposal. Conversely, retaining explicit unsupported outcomes does not by
itself require moving ocean upstream. Exact chosen versions and persistence compatibility remain
separately reviewed C1 work.

Polar and fragmentation already belong to macro. Their concrete paired failures require honest
control failure reporting and bounded geometry/placement successors before claiming their
realization. Broader single-owner support is an optional representation extension with new
capacity proofs; relaxing the existing chart theorem or silently narrowing public inputs is not
authorized. Sample-surviving detached components/channels and two-way mapped boundary/role
certification remain distinct D1 work. The previously rejected visual family is not rescued by
these numeric checks. The bounded D3 pass and replay are complete; no additional seed scan,
semantic rerun on favorable masks, constructor repair or new profile is part of this result.

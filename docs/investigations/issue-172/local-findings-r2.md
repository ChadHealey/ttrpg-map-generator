## Paid islands

The second recipe replaces the first recipe's coast-aligned radial slivers with three explicit
compact irregular polygons of six or seven vertices. Every member chooses a shape and orientation
from its own seed/owner/category/member/layout/variant stream. Orientation is independent of the
coast normal. Unequal category weights are now `1 / (1 + 1.2 * index)^1.4`, so larger members remain
visibly distinct from their smaller companions.

Each category still pays its exact independent share from owner quota. Zero abundance removes
only that category. Actual polygon area is fitted directly, then the polygon is rotated and
translated without changing its share. The actual oriented polygon's bounding radius determines
its outward offset from the selected coast edge. Every member still has six fixed coast anchors
and four outward offsets: `.015, .035, .065, .1` in post-fit chart units. Actual body, bay and
prior-island contacts reject the site.

The four candidate variants permit island vertices to extend at most `[0, .025, .05, .09]`
beyond the body's maximum vertex radius. This is an explicit compactness preference, not a
certificate change. The larger final margin is needed for the full eleven-member small-owner
inventory; exhaustion remains an explicit failure. Distinct layout/variant site streams avoid
repeating the same four subordinate placement attempts across all twelve slots. No island is
silently dropped or made unpaid.
